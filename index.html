Travel & Stay ✦

🚕 Getting There

Hampton Court House is a 35-minute train ride from London Waterloo to Hampton Court Station, followed by a short 10-minute walk through the grounds.

🏨 Where to Stay

We recommend staying in Hampton Court, Kingston upon Thames, or Richmond. Nearby options include The Mitre Hotel, Carlton Mitre, and Premier Inn Kingston.
