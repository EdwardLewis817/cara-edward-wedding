Our Story

How it all began

Every great love story has a beginning. Ours is one we'll be telling for the rest of our lives — full of laughter, a little luck, and the quiet certainty that we had found exactly the right person.

After years together building a life, a home, and countless memories, Edward got down on one knee and asked the question we'd both known was coming — and everything changed for the better.

On the 7th of August 2027, surrounded by the people we love most, we will make it official at the beautiful Hampton Court House.

We cannot wait to celebrate with you.

— Cara & Edward

The Venue

Hampton Court House

Hampton Court Road
East Molesey
Surrey, KT8 9BS

A stunning Georgian mansion set within nine acres of private grounds, overlooking the ancient oaks of Bushy Park. Hampton Court House is an intimate and extraordinary venue — exclusive to us and our guests for the entire day.

The nearest train station is Hampton Court, with direct services from London Waterloo. On-site parking is also available for guests travelling by car.
